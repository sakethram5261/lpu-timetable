📱 How to Add on iPhone / iOS

  • 1
    Open this page in Safari on your iPhone

    Tap the orange "Subscribe on iPhone" button above.

  • 2
    Confirm Subscription

    iOS will pop up a prompt asking to subscribe to "LPU Timetable". Tap Subscribe.

  • 3
    Set Auto-Refresh

    Ensure Auto-Refresh is set to Every Day or Every Hour, then tap Add.

🌐 How to Add to Google Calendar

  • 1
    Copy the Subscription Link

    Click the "Copy Link" button in the box above.

  • 2
    Open Google Calendar (Web)

    On the left sidebar, click the + next to "Other calendars" and select From URL.

  • 3
    Paste and Add

    Paste the copied URL and click Add calendar.
